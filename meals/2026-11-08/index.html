Cajun Sausage and Rice Skillet

45 min · one skillet · Budget Bytes

Ingredients

  • 1 medium organic green bell pepper
  • 14 oz North Country andouille sausage (of 24 oz bought; freeze the other 10 oz)
  • 1 can (14.5 oz) 365 fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 1.5 cups chicken broth from on-hand bouillon
  • 2 green onions (on hand)
  • 1 Tbsp cooking oil / olive oil
  • 1 cup dry (~185 g) jasmine rice (on hand)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne
  • 1/8 tsp black pepper

Steps

  1. Brown the sausage.Slice 14 oz andouille 1/4–1/2 inch thick. Brown in 1 Tbsp oil in a deep skillet over medium heat.
    14 oz andouille sausage1 Tbsp oil
  2. Add the pepper.Dice 1 medium green bell pepper while the sausage cooks. Add and sauté 1 min.
    1 green bell pepper
  3. Toast the spices.Add 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1/2 tsp oregano, 1/2 tsp thyme, 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1/4 tsp onion powder, 1/8 tsp cayenne and 1/8 tsp black pepper. Sauté 1 min to toast.
    1 tsp smoked paprika1/2 tsp dried oregano1/2 tsp dried thyme1/4 tsp garlic powder1/4 tsp onion powder1/8 tsp cayenne1/8 tsp black pepper
  4. Add tomatoes, rice and broth.Add the whole 14.5 oz fire-roasted tomato can with juices, 1 cup dry jasmine rice and 1.5 cups chicken broth (dissolve on-hand chicken bouillon in water per package directions). Stir and loosen browned bits.
    1 can fire-roasted tomatoes1 cup dry jasmine rice1.5 cups chicken broth (bouillon)
  5. Simmer and rest.Cover and bring to a full boil over medium-high. Reduce to low; simmer 20 min. Remove from heat and rest, covered, 5 min.
    No additional ingredients
  6. Finish.Fold gently to redistribute; top with 2 sliced green onions (on hand).
    2 green onions
  7. Serve and store.Serve two plates. Cool the other two promptly in shallow containers; refrigerate within 2 hours (rice within 1–2 hours). Reheat Tue Nov 10 to 165°F.
    No additional ingredients

Ground Beef Bowl

30 min · Healthy Fitness Meals — Rena Awada

Ingredients

  • 16 oz 365 90% lean ground beef (1 pack)
  • 2 large (~700 g) organic sweet potatoes, peeled, ¼-inch cubes
  • 1 large organic Hass avocado
  • 2 cups (16 oz tub) organic low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1 packet (1.3 oz) Siete mild taco seasoning
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil, divided (on hand)
  • 1 Tbsp tomato paste (on hand)
  • 3 Tbsp hot honey, divided (on hand)
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon (on hand)
  • To taste salt, pepper, crushed red pepper (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat the oven.Heat to 350°F; line a sheet pan with parchment.
    Sheet pan + parchment
  2. Season the sweet potatoes.Peel and cut 2 large sweet potatoes (~700 g) into 1/4-inch cubes. Toss with 1 Tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp cinnamon, salt and pepper.
    2 large sweet potatoes (~700 g)1 Tbsp olive oil1 tsp cinnamonsalt and pepper
  3. Roast.Roast the 700 g seasoned sweet potatoes for 18–20 min, until cooked through and crisp at the edges.
    Seasoned sweet potatoes
  4. Brown and season the beef.Meanwhile heat the remaining 1 Tbsp olive oil. Brown 16 oz 90/10 beef, breaking it up (source suggests 2–3 min). Add 1 packet taco seasoning (1.3 oz) and 1 Tbsp tomato paste; cook 2 min more.
    1 Tbsp olive oil16 oz ground beef1 packet taco seasoning1 Tbsp tomato paste
  5. Add hot honey.Stir 1 Tbsp hot honey into the 16 oz seasoned beef. Add a splash of water if dry. Continue until ground beef reaches 160°F; time alone is not a safety check.
    1 Tbsp hot honey
  6. Assemble four bowls.Across four plates divide 700 g roasted potatoes, 16 oz cooked-from-raw beef, 1 large avocado and 2 cups cottage cheese (1/2 cup each). Drizzle remaining 2 Tbsp hot honey (1/2 Tbsp each). Optional chili flakes to taste.
    Roasted sweet potatoesCooked beef1 avocado, sliced2 cups cottage cheese2 Tbsp hot honey
  7. Serve and save Wednesday’s plates.Mon Nov 9: serve two bowls. Chill remaining two portions of beef/potatoes within 2 hr; store 1/2 avocado and 1 cup cottage cheese separately. Wed Nov 11: reheat beef/potatoes to 165°F, then add cold toppings.
    No additional ingredients
